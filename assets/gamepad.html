Pairing help

Xbox: Turn on the controller and hold its pairing button until the Xbox light flashes quickly. A Bluetooth-capable model is required.

Switch Pro / compatible controllers: Hold the SYNC button until the player lights cycle. Compatibility depends on the controller and installed Linux driver.

Button bindings

Disable gamepad control, select Bind next to a function, then press one controller button. Bindings are saved on Duck and survive restarts. Binding a button does not run the action.

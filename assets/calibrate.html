ARDUINO UNO Q

Zero calibration

ControlCalibrationServo setup

After the robot is wired, start calibration. Temporary gains become KP = 3 and KD = 0, and every servo is commanded to 2047.

Procedure

Policy and pose control stop while this page is active. Drag a slider to move one joint (raw encoder 0–4095). Place the joint at its mechanical zero, then press Finish. The current encoder reading is stored as that joint's zero. A green row means Finish was pressed during this session. Leaving or restarting clears that highlight. It is not written into the saved zero by itself.

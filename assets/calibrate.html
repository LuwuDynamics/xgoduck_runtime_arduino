Unified calibration

Servo centers → Neck (ID 30) → IMU → Default pose check

Entering calibration and unloading all servos…

01

Set the servo centers

    Unload the servos before moving the joints by hand. Keep the wires clear of the gaps between the legs and body.

    02

    Calibrate the neck servo (ID 30) separately

    With the servos unloaded, position the neck parallel to the torso as shown. Click Calibrate ID 30, then unload it before calibrating the IMU.

    03

    IMU calibration

    Unload the servos and support Duck with the board upright, the battery connector pointing up and the board’s bottom edge against a level table. Keep Duck still until calibration finishes.

    Waiting for IMU feedback…

    04

    Check the default pose

    When calibration is complete, support Duck on a level surface, click Default pose and compare its stance with the photo.

    If the pose differs from the photo, support Duck and click Unload all. Recheck the servo centers

    If the centers are correct, a servo may be installed at the wrong joint. Open Servo setup, select an ID on the 3D model, and click Start test. Move the slider slightly and check that the moving joint matches the highlighted one. When done, click Stop and unload.

    If the servos are installed correctly and the pose is still off, use the guide below to recalibrate the affected joint.

    Single-servo calibration

    Support Duck. Find the selected servo in the photo and position its unloaded joint by hand to match the diagram. After setting its center, you can move on to the next unloaded joint.

    Orange marks the joint. Align it with the green dashed line. Red shows a misaligned position.

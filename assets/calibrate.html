Set the servo centers

    Unload the servos before moving the joints by hand. Keep the wires clear of the gaps between the legs and body.

    IMU calibration

    Unload the servos and support Duck with the board upright, the battery connector pointing up and the board’s bottom edge against a level table. Start calibration and hold still for about 1.5 seconds.

    Waiting for IMU feedback…

    03

    Check the default pose

    When calibration is complete, support Duck on a level surface, click Default pose and compare its stance with the photo.

    If the pose differs, support Duck and click Unload all before rechecking the servo centers.
